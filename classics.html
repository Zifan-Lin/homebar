House
Classics

The drinks that never go out of season
Strong & Short
Neat or on the rocks

Spirit-forward, lightly diluted. Sip slowly.

Old Fashioned
Strong
Bourbon or rye, barely sweetened, bitters-kissed, finished with an orange peel. The oldest cocktail worth ordering. No muddled fruit.
2 oz bourbon or rye 1 tsp simple syrup 2 dashes Angostura orange peel large ice cube
Negroni
Strong
Equal parts gin, sweet vermouth, and Campari. Bitter, herbal, deeply red. The most complete cocktail ever assembled from three ingredients.
1 oz gin 1 oz sweet vermouth 1 oz Campari orange peel
Manhattan
Strong
Rye and sweet vermouth, stirred long and cold, finished with a cherry. Smoother than its sibling the Old Fashioned, with a quieter kind of power.
2 oz rye whiskey 1 oz sweet vermouth 2 dashes Angostura brandied cherry
Martini
Strong
Gin and dry vermouth, stirred until very cold, served up with an olive or a twist. Specify your preference — this is not a drink that tolerates ambiguity.
2½ oz gin ½ oz dry vermouth olive or lemon twist
Bright & Balanced
Shaken, served up or short

Citrus-forward, medium strength. The evening's middle ground.

Margarita
Medium
Tequila, triple sec, fresh lime. Salt rim optional but recommended. Simple enough to make badly; good enough to make very well.
2 oz blanco tequila ¾ oz triple sec ¾ oz fresh lime juice salted rim
Whiskey Sour
Medium
Bourbon, lemon juice, a little sweetness. Tart and warming in equal measure. Ask about the egg white — it changes everything.
2 oz bourbon ¾ oz fresh lemon juice ½ oz simple syrup egg white (optional)
Daiquiri
Medium
White rum, fresh lime, simple syrup — shaken hard and served up cold. Nothing frozen. The original is better than what the blender made you think.
2 oz white rum ¾ oz fresh lime juice ¾ oz simple syrup
Gimlet
Medium
Gin and fresh lime juice, lightly sweetened. Crisp, clean, unforgiving of bad gin. One of the shortest distances between two points in cocktail history.
2 oz gin ¾ oz fresh lime juice ¾ oz simple syrup
Long & Easy
Tall glass, lower ABV

Built over ice, lengthened with soda or wine. Pace yourself, or don't.

Gin & Tonic
Light
London dry gin, tonic water, lime. The ratio matters more than the recipe. A well-made G&T is always the right decision.
2 oz London dry gin 4 oz tonic water lime wedge tall glass, plenty of ice
Mojito
Light
White rum, fresh lime, mint, sugar, soda. Refreshing to make, refreshing to drink. Requires actual fresh mint — no substitutions accepted.
2 oz white rum ¾ oz fresh lime juice 2 tsp sugar 8–10 mint leaves soda water to top
Moscow Mule
Light
Vodka, ginger beer, lime — traditionally served in a copper mug that keeps it impossibly cold. The ginger does most of the heavy lifting. Good ginger beer matters.
2 oz vodka ½ oz fresh lime juice 4 oz ginger beer lime wheel
Aperol Spritz
Light
Aperol, prosecco, a splash of soda. Bittersweet, bright orange, relentlessly cheerful. Best drunk in a large wine glass with too much ice.
3 oz prosecco 2 oz Aperol 1 oz soda water orange slice
